# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, senior designers, and founders across tech — anyone in a position to bring Prerna in or refer her. They're scanning multiple portfolios in one sitting, on desktop, under time pressure. They need to feel the designer's personality within seconds and trust her craft within minutes.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Prerna Kashyap, a product designer with a background in biotech and customer success. The site communicates who she is as much as what she's made. Success = a visitor finishing the homepage with a clear sense of her as a person, wanting to read a case study, and feeling confident she's worth reaching out to.

## Brand Personality

Playful, smart, considered. Curious by nature. Reads the room. Warm but not soft — there's rigor underneath the personality. The "eldest daughter in an Asian family" energy: capable, self-aware, slightly wry.

## Anti-references

Generic Webflow portfolios: cream or warm-neutral background, floating border-radius cards, oversized sans-serif hero headings, timeline-style case study layout. The 2024–25 designer portfolio default that reads as "I followed a YouTube tutorial." This portfolio should not fit that mould.

## Design Principles

1. **Personality is the differentiator.** The work is good, but so is everyone's. The voice, the specificity, and the wit are what make her memorable. Design should amplify that, not sand it down.
2. **Show, don't announce.** The portfolio itself is the proof of craft. Every spacing decision, every typographic choice is a signal. No section needs to say "I care about details" — it should be felt.
3. **Earn every element.** Nothing on the page that doesn't carry its weight. No decorative cards, no filler copy, no motion for motion's sake.
4. **Warmth through specificity.** "Let's grab some boba" is better than "Let's connect." Specific beats generic at every scale — copy, imagery, details.
5. **The visitor is busy.** Clarity and hierarchy over richness. The hierarchy should do the reading for them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the floor. Custom cursor must not be the only interaction affordance. Reduced motion preference must be respected. No content gated behind animation.
